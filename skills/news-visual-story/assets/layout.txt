[LAYOUT — fixed]
Landscape 16:9, 1920 by 1080, generous safe margins on all edges. Top-left reading block: a small orange date badge, then a compact entity line with an optional series marker, then a bold left-aligned headline of one or two lines. When a summary paragraph is given, it sits directly under the headline in a clearly smaller but fully legible size, at most three lines, left-aligned. When a deck is given, it sits under the headline or summary as one line. The visual field occupies roughly 55 to 65 percent of the canvas, center and right; it must show the relation named in the STORY block, never merely decorate the text. Text and visual form one composition: labels, value labels and glossary notes sit on or beside the elements they describe, never in a detached column. Footnote bottom-left, small. Source tag bottom-right, small. One dominant reading path: headline, then summary or deck, then the visual with its labels, then footnote and source. All glyphs are controlled only by the TEXT block.
